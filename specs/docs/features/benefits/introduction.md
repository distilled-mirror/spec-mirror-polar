# Automated Benefits

Polar offers built-in benefit (entitlements) automation for common upsells within the developer &
designer ecosystem with more to come.

* [**Credits**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/credits.md). A simple benefit that allows you to credit a customer's Usage Meter balance.
* [**License Keys**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/license-keys.md). Software license keys that you can customize the branding of.
* [**Feature Flags**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/feature-flags.md). Simple, API-driven feature access flags with optional metadata.
* [**File Downloads**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/file-downloads.md). Downloadable files of any kind up to 10GB each.
* [**GitHub Repository Access**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/github-access.md). Automatically invite subscribers to private GitHub repo(s).
* [**Discord Invite**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/discord-access.md). Automate invitations and granting of roles to subscribers and customers.
* [**Shared Slack Channel**](https://polar.sh/docs/features/benefits/slack-shared-channel.md). Give customers a shared Slack channel via Slack Connect.

## Product & Subscription Benefits

Product and subscription benefits are standalone resources in Polar - connected to one or many products or subscription tiers.

This approach is a bit different from other platforms, but offers many advantages:

* Easy to enable the same benefit across multiple products & subscriptions
* You can change a benefit in one place vs. many
* No duplicate data or work (error prone)
* More intuitive UI for you and your customers

**How customers get access to benefits:**

* ✅ Active subscribers of tiers with the benefit enabled
* ✅ Customers who bought a product with the benefit (lifetime access)
* ❌ Subscribers with an expired subscription (cancelled)
* ❌ Users who are not customers

## Creating & Managing Benefits

You can manage benefits in two ways:

1. Directly within a product create/edit form
2. Or via `Benefits` in your dashboard
