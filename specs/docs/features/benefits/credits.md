# Credits Benefit

> Create your own Credits benefit

The Credits benefit allows you to credit a customer's Usage Meter balance.

![](https://mintcdn.com/polar/Ut0vPUvE1pIdMcH2/assets/features/benefits/credits/credits.light.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=Ut0vPUvE1pIdMcH2\&q=85\&s=95a964aa562069bd58a551e44c757459)

## Crediting Usage Meter Balance

The Credits benefit will credit a customer's Usage Meter balance at different points in time depending on the type of product purchased.

### Subscription Products

The customer will be credited the amount of units specified in the benefit at the beginning of every subscription cycle period — monthly or yearly.

If the product has a [meter cycle](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/billing.md#meter-cycle), credits are granted at the start of every meter period instead. For example, a yearly plan with a monthly meter cycle grants credits every month.

### One-Time Products

The customer will be credited the amount of units specified in the benefit once at the time of purchase.

## Rollover unused credits

You can choose to rollover unused credits to the next billing cycle. This means that if a customer doesn't use all of their credits in a given billing cycle, the remaining credits will be added to their balance for the next billing cycle. With a [meter cycle](https://polar.sh/docs/features/usage-based-billing/billing.md#meter-cycle), unused credits roll over to the next meter period. To enable this feature, check the "Rollover unused credits" checkbox when creating or editing the Credits benefit.

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you change the rollover setting for a benefit, it will only apply to new
> credits issued after the change. Existing credits will not be affected.
